**Positioning:**
RelayDesk AI is a workflow assistant for small ecommerce support teams, designed to streamline ticket responses, reduce first-response times, and increase team efficiency.

**Audience:**
Primary buyers are ecommerce founders and support operations leads at teams with 5 to 50 employees, seeking to improve support operations and reduce repetitive tasks.

**Channels:**

1. **Content Marketing:** Create informative blog posts, case studies, and whitepapers highlighting the benefits of RelayDesk AI for small ecommerce support teams. (Weeks 1-4)
2. **Social Media:** Utilize LinkedIn and Twitter to share engaging content, industry insights, and product updates. (Weeks 1-4)
3. **Email Marketing:** Send targeted email campaigns to potential customers, showcasing RelayDesk AI's features and benefits. (Weeks 1-4)

**Weeks 1-4:**

* Week 1: Establish content marketing presence, launch social media campaigns, and send introductory email campaigns.
* Week 2: Publish case studies and whitepapers, and engage with potential customers through social media and email.
* Week 3: Optimize content marketing and email campaigns based on early pilot data, and launch paid social media ads.
