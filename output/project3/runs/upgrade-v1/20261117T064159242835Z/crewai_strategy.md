**30-Day Launch Plan for RelayDesk AI**

**Positioning:**
RelayDesk AI is a workflow assistant for small ecommerce support teams, designed to streamline ticket responses, reduce response times, and increase team efficiency. Our solution drafts ticket replies, retrieves inventory and policy answers, and creates campaign briefs, ensuring accuracy and reliability.

**Audience:**
Primary buyers are ecommerce founders and support operations leads at teams with 5 to 50 employees, seeking to improve their support operations and reduce the time spent on repetitive tasks.

**Channels:**

1. **Email**: Targeted campaigns to the identified audience, highlighting the benefits of RelayDesk AI and its ability to draft ticket replies, retrieve inventory and policy answers, and create campaign briefs.
2. **Social Media**: Utilize LinkedIn and Twitter to share success stories, tips, and best practices related to support operations and workflow automation.
3. **Content Marketing**: Create informative blog posts, whitepapers, and case studies to educate the target audience on the benefits of RelayDesk AI and its capabilities.

**Weeks 1-4:**

Week 1: Awareness and Education

* Launch email campaign targeting the identified audience, introducing RelayDesk AI and its benefits.
* Share a blog post on "5 Ways to Streamline Your Support Operations" on LinkedIn and Twitter.

Week 2: Engagement and Lead Generation

* Share a case study on a successful implementation of RelayDesk AI, highlighting the benefits and results.
* Host a webinar on "Maximizing Support Efficiency with RelayDesk AI" and invite the target audience to participate.

Week 3: Conversion and Trials

* Offer a free trial of RelayDesk AI to a select group of potential customers, allowing them to experience the benefits firsthand.
* Share testimonials and success stories from early adopters on social media and email campaigns.

Week 4: Retention and Expansion

* Follow up with trial participants to gather feedback and encourage adoption.
* Share a whitepaper on "The Future of Support Operations: How AI Can Help" to educate the target audience on the benefits of RelayDesk AI.

**Owners:**

* Marketing: Responsible for executing the email, social media, and content marketing campaigns.
* Sales: Responsible for managing the free trial and follow-up with potential customers.
* Product: Responsible for ensuring the quality and accuracy of RelayDesk AI's outputs.

**Proposed KPIs:**

1. Ticket deflection rate
2. Median first-response time
3. Answer correction rate
4. Qualified demos
5. Trial activation
6. Customer acquisition cost
7. Percentage of AI drafts approved without edits

**Budget:**
The marketing spend for the 30-day launch will be tracked and monitored to ensure it remains below $12,000. A detailed breakdown of the budget is as follows:

* Email campaign: $4,000
* Social media advertising: $3,000
* Content creation: $2,000
* Webinar hosting: $1,000
* Free trial management: $1,000
* Miscellaneous (tracking and monitoring): $1,000

**Risks and Mitigations:**

1. Risk: Unrealistic expectations from the target audience.
Mitigation: Clearly communicate the capabilities and limitations of RelayDesk AI to the target audience.
2. Risk: Insufficient budget for marketing spend.
Mitigation: Monitor and adjust the marketing spend to ensure it remains within the allocated budget.
