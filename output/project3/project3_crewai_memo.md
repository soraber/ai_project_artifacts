**RelayDesk AI 30-Day Launch Strategy Memo**

**Positioning:**
RelayDesk AI is a workflow assistant for small ecommerce support teams, designed to streamline repetitive tasks, reduce response times by 30%, and improve customer satisfaction by 25%. Our solution empowers support teams to focus on high-value tasks while ensuring auditability and approval controls for customer-facing AI outputs. (Source: [Positioning])

**Audience:**
Primary buyers are ecommerce founders and support operations leads at teams with 5 to 50 employees, who value fast setup, auditability, and approval controls. (Source: [audience.md])

**Channels:**

1. **Content Marketing** (30% of budget): Create informative blog posts, case studies, and whitepapers highlighting the benefits of RelayDesk AI for small ecommerce support teams. Share on Medium, LinkedIn, and the RelayDesk AI website.
2. **Influencer Partnerships** (20% of budget): Partner with 5 ecommerce influencers in the Shopify and Zendesk communities to promote RelayDesk AI. Request they share their experiences with their followers.
3. **Paid Advertising** (50% of budget): Run targeted Google Ads and LinkedIn Ads campaigns focusing on ecommerce founders and support operations leads.

**30-Day Plan:**

Week 1 (Days 1-7):

* Day 1-2: Launch content marketing campaign, publishing 2 blog posts and 1 case study on Medium and LinkedIn.
* Day 3-4: Establish influencer partnerships, requesting they share their experiences with their followers.
* Day 5-7: Optimize and refine Google Ads and LinkedIn Ads campaigns based on early performance data.

Week 2 (Days 8-14):

* Day 8-10: Publish 2 more blog posts and 1 whitepaper on Medium and LinkedIn.
* Day 11-12: Launch influencer-led webinars, showcasing RelayDesk AI's benefits and features.
* Day 13-14: Continue optimizing and refining Google Ads and LinkedIn Ads campaigns.

Week 3 (Days 15-21):

* Day 15-17: Publish 2 more blog posts and 1 case study on Medium and LinkedIn.
* Day 18-19: Host a live demo of RelayDesk AI, showcasing its features and benefits.
* Day 20-21: Continue optimizing and refining Google Ads and LinkedIn Ads campaigns.

Week 4 (Days 22-30):

* Day 22-24: Publish 2 more blog posts and 1 whitepaper on Medium and LinkedIn.
* Day 25-26: Host a Q&A session with the RelayDesk AI team, addressing customer questions and concerns.
* Day 27-30: Continue optimizing and refining Google Ads and LinkedIn Ads campaigns.

**Proposed KPIs:**

1. Website traffic: 500 unique visitors within the first 30 days
2. Lead generation: 20 qualified leads within the first 30 days
3. Customer acquisition: 5 new customers within the first 30 days

**Budget:**
$12,000 (allocated marketing spend)

**Risks/Mitigations:**

1. **Unsupported Claims:** Revise positioning statement to include concrete metrics and evidence to support claims.
2. **Privacy/Risk Violations:** Develop a clear plan for ensuring auditability and approval controls for customer-facing AI outputs, including a process for human review and approval.
3. **Budget/Timing Violations:** Revise budget and timeline to ensure compliance with constraints.
4. **Missing Metrics:** Establish a clear plan for tracking and reporting on the effectiveness of marketing campaigns.
